About the project

AI-Powered Smart QR-Based College Navigation System

Large campuses are hard to navigate for visitors, parents, new students and recruiters. This system lets anyone scan a QR code at the entrance and get an instant, voice-guided walking route to any department — no app install required.

Scan & Go

QR codes at the gate and key junctions open the site instantly in any phone browser.

Shortest Path

Routes follow real walkways using a graph-based shortest-path algorithm, not straight lines through buildings.

Voice Guided

Turn-by-turn spoken directions in multiple languages help elderly and visually impaired visitors too.
